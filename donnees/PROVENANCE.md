# Wind data provenance

The file `wind.txt.gz` is the `wind` dataset distributed with the CRAN source archive NPCirc 3.2.1, listed by CRAN as published 2026-02-20: https://cran.r-project.org/package=NPCirc. Its documentation is retained as `wind_provenance.Rd`.

Original data: Texas Natural Resources Conservation Commission (2008), Texas Natural Resources Conservation Commission (TNRCC), version 1.0, NSF NCAR Earth Observing Laboratory, https://doi.org/10.26023/ZQEZ-ENSF-T09. The C28_1 series covers 20 May to 31 July 2003. UCAR's public metadata for dataset 85.034 lists access constraints and legal constraints as none: https://data.ucar.edu/zh_Hant_TW/dataset/texas-natural-resources-conservation-commission-tnrcc. No more specific data licence was identified. NPCirc itself is GPL (>=2); the package licence must not be automatically equated with a separate licence for the underlying data.

NCAR EOL states a policy of open data sharing and requests acknowledgment and a data citation for its data: https://www.eol.ucar.edu/data-software/data-services/eol-data-policy. The manuscript includes an acknowledgement and the dataset DOI.
